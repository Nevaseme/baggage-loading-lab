- Authoritative status was `is_included=true`, `is_valid=false`, `is_placed_safe=false`. The environment does not place the item after a failed transport-path check, so `is_valid` is the primary failure and the false safety flag is downstream.
- Replay of the saved pre-action snapshot captured the exact route: MPC returned no candidate, depth-aware emergency returned no candidate, then the unchecked deterministic last resort produced the failed action.

Changing analytic path clearance from 18 mm to the official 15 mm still produced zero strict candidates. This indicates the policy likely created a support/ingress dead-end before step 14, not merely a missing endpoint coordinate.
- A fused-family shadow scan then exact-masked up to 2,500 normal and roughly 2,056--2,500 dense/free-boundary rescue proposals for each of all ten visible pool items. It found zero strict roots for every item. The scanned coordinates covered floor/wall, support lattice, obstacle faces, support edges, legacy crosses, free boundaries, and dense support grids.
